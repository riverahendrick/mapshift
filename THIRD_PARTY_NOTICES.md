https://github.com/tree-sitter/tree-sitter

## Language grammars

The published package bundles 19 tree-sitter grammars (TypeScript, TSX, JavaScript, Python, Go, Java,
Kotlin, C#, Rust, PHP, Dart, Swift, Ruby, Scala, Lua, Elixir, PowerShell, C and C++) and their tag
queries, taken from the tree-sitter-wasm collection (MIT License). Each grammar keeps the open-source
license of its own project; the full notice travels with the grammars in `dist/grammars/NOTICE.md`.
https://github.com/Crysthamus/tree-sitter-wasm
